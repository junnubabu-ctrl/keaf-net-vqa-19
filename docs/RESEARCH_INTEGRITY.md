# Research integrity controls

- No benchmark number is a fact until regenerated from a versioned run artifact.
- Paper 3 manuscript numbers remain unverified until separately reproduced.
- No gold answer or test label may enter retrieval, verification, calibration, or inference.
- Negative and null results remain in the run registry.
- Result tables and figures must be generated from machine-readable outputs, never hand-edited.
- No similarity-detector or AI-detector evasion target is part of the work.
- Authorship, acknowledgements, funding, conflicts, and availability statements must be factual.
- Human evaluation requires prior approval, disclosed sampling, at least two blinded annotators,
  adjudication, agreement reporting, and appropriate consent/data handling.
